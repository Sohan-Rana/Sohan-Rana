## About

I'm a Software Engineering student at Daffodil International University, Dhaka. I work where AI, Human-Computer Interaction and Bangla NLP meet real human needs, always asking: *who does this technology actually help?*

I'm Co-founder & COO of [Honqio IT](https://honqioit.netlify.app) (Cloud, AI, DevOps, Data Science), and I've published research on an Arduino-based farm intrusion alert system.

## Projects

- **BanglaJobGuard**: explainable hybrid AI that flags fraudulent job ads in Bangladesh. Rule-based MVP is live; NLP/ML models are next.
- **EarthLens**: NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 (Team Cosmic Mindshift). Turns NASA Earth data into temperature and rainfall insights for Bangladesh.
- **[Smart Field Alert System](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/404114451_Smart_Field_Alert_System_Using_Arduino_for_Agricultural_Intrusion_Detection)**: low-cost PIR sensor intrusion detection for farmland. Published.
